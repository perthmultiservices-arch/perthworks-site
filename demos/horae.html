The Movement

Every gear is finished by one pair of hands.

Our movements are assembled, adjusted, and signed by a single watchmaker from start to finish — not a production line. If something feels alive under the caseback, that's why.

The Fit

Worn so often it forgets it's being worn.

The lug geometry was redrawn nine times until the case disappeared into the wrist. Horae is designed to be noticed by others, and forgotten by its owner.

The Dial

Grand feu enamel, fired four times.

A single crack in the firing and the dial is destroyed — which is why most workshops stopped doing it. We didn't. Depth you can't get from printing is worth the risk.
