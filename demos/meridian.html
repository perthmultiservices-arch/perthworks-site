The Hull

Shaped against real waves, not simulated ones.

Every hull spends four weeks in open-water trials off Rottnest before it's signed off. Software can model a wave. It can't model what it feels like at 2am in a swell — only the ocean can test that.

The Deck

Teak laid by the same two hands, every time.

Each plank is fitted and caulked by the same joiner across the whole build, so the grain reads as one continuous surface rather than a hundred separate pieces.

The Cabin

Quiet enough to hear the water, not the engine.

Triple-insulated bulkheads keep the cabin at library-level quiet underway — so what you hear at anchor is the sea against the hull, not anything mechanical.
