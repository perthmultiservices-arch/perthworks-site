The Body

Paint that catches the light, not just the eye.

Eleven layers, hand-flowed over carbon. Each panel is wet-sanded between coats until the surface reads less like a car and more like still water — so every streetlight leaves a trail across it as you pass.

No two Veloce finishes are identical. That's not a limitation. That's the point.

The Details

Every edge was argued over for a month.

The lamp housing took fourteen prototypes. Not because it needed to look different — because it needed to look inevitable, like it was never going to be anything else.

The Cabin

Built around your hands, not the other way around.

Every Veloce interior is fitted in person, to its owner, before a single stitch is cut. The result is a cabin that feels less like a cockpit and more like a tailored jacket doing 300 km/h.
