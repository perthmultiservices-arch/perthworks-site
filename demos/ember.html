The Tasting

Nine courses. No menu printed in advance.

What arrives depends on what the fire allowed that day. We plan the structure — smoke, char, ash, ember — and let the market and the coals fill in the rest.

The Kitchen

You eat at the counter. The fire is the view.

There's no pass, no hidden kitchen. Fourteen seats wrap the hearth so every guest watches their own plate get built, three feet away, in heat that makes the air shimmer.

The Room

Lit almost entirely by the coals themselves.

We run two candles and the hearth. Everything else is shadow — on purpose. It's the only lighting plan that makes the food look exactly like it tastes.
